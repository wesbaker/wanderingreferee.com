date: '2026-08-11'
description: Characters who sometimes find tact and sometimes lose it make for more interesting stories than those who stay reliably composed.
tags: ["rpgaday", "rpgs"]
---
As a GM, I love seeing PCs who slip into and out of having ==tact==. Maybe it's the barbarian who holds his tongue in negotiations or the courtier who loses their temper when the negotiations remind them of a previous failure. Keeps it fresh.
