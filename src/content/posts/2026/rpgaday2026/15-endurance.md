---
title: "#RPGaDay2026 Day 15: Endurance"
date: '2026-08-15'
description: New GMs should plan for shorter sessions and arcs than they expect; player endurance and long campaigns both have limits.
tags: ["rpgaday", "rpgs"]
---
My advice to new GMs is not to assume your players have the ==Endurance== to go for long sessions or arcs. Assume two-ish hours and prepare for ways to cut, pause, and resume later. Also, long campaign arcs are the ones that always get shortened. Plan for less.
