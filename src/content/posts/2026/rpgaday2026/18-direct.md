title: "#RPGaDay2026 Day 18: Direct"
date: '2026-08-18'
description: Be direct with your fellow players and GM about what you want; no one can reliably guess and make it happen for you.
tags: ["rpgaday", "rpgs"]
---
Be ==Direct== with your players and your GM. The more you hold back, the less you're going to get. Don't be a doormat and say what you want. You might not get it, but no one is going to magically guess what you want and make it happen for you. You've got to say the thing aloud.

This advice holds for everything in life, actually.
