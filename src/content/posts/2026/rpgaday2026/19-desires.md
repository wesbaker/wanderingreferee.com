What ==Desires== do your players have? How does that help you design the adventures you run? Should you always give them their desires? Probably, but never as *directly* as they'd have otherwise.

My Shadowdark group wants to revive a slime they found as a pet. I'm good with that, but they're going to have to quest for it. They were fairly sure they could make it happen in a session. I'm going to make it a full arc where they have to work with a disembodied sorcerer.

Meet their desires, but make them work for it. Getting what you want instantly is boring. It's certainly not in the hero's journey.
