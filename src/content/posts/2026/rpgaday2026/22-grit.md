title: "#RPGaDay2026 Day 22: Grit"
date: '2026-08-22'
description: With so many games and adventures available, consider whether perseverance is the only reason to continue something that is not working.
tags: ["rpgaday", "rpgs"]
---
When we are flush for choice when it comes to RPGs and adventures, we should consider how much ==Grit== factors into our decision to keep playing a given adventure or system.

The cost of changing adventures is cheap, systems less so, but don't feel stuck or beholden just because you started the thing. Chat with your group and find a way out of something that's not working. Don't take on the mindset of finishing everything you started. Life is too short for that.
