As both a player of board games and RPGs, ==Familiarity== with the rules is the ultimate goal, because when that happens they fall away and play takes center stage.

I'm finding that for me, there's a sweet spot of rules and that exists around the level of Shadowdark or OSE. Both make some intuitive sense and can fit in my head. I haven't played a ton of either, but there's no frequently forgotten rules that I need a reminder on.

I still like heavier systems too (Pathfinder 2e, namely), but there's only so much time to invest into these games and I can only fit so many heavier games into my repertoire.
