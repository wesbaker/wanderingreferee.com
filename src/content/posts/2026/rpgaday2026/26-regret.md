date: '2026-08-26'
description: I once feared missing out on every RPG, but now I'd regret spending all my time evaluating games instead of actually playing one.
tags: ["rpgaday", "rpgs"]
---
When I first got into RPGs, I wanted to try them all. I had a sense that I'd ==Regret== not trying everything and throwing myself entirely into this hobby.

Now, I'd ==Regret== not dedicating some real time to playing a game instead of constantly evaluating the many games on offer. The whole point is playing. If you're constantly looking for better, you're not doing the thing you want to do (or you're lying to yourself).
