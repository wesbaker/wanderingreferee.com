Ever since seeing [3x5 Arcana's post on doors, "The Door Tax"](https://blog.3x5arcana.com/p/the-door-tax), and specifically Ostius, the god of doors, I've been thinking about how I can use ==Devotion== to deities to shape my game.

Ostius as written provides XP or Luck for opening doors "without delay." That nudge means what could be a long, drawn-out question of how to open a door and whether it's trapped doesn't happen at all. Instead, everyone wants to just open the door.

I almost always want to encourage being impulsive. Perhaps we could expand Ostius into the god of curiosity: opening doors, sure, but also looking in pits, opening chests, pulling levers, and more.
