==Labour== brings up two ideas for me: downtime and hirelings.

On downtime, ==Labour== is something newer players can do to earn some extra coin between adventures, but probably not much, so I typically ignore it.

For hirelings, they're all ==labourers==, hired help that stays to help because they're paid to. But that's why we have morale rolls to see if they stick around. My group has a torchbearer named Giuseppe, and we'll see how long he sticks around after they keep making fun of him…
